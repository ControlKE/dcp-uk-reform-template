UK Chapter Priorities

What DCP UK is raising with the national party on behalf of Kenyans in Britain.

✅01

Diaspora voting rights

Kenyan citizens abroad have a constitutional right to vote, but in practice registration and polling have been available in only a small number of countries. The chapter collects members’ experience of trying to register and vote from the UK, and argues for arrangements that work in practice.

  • Registration points that are reachable from outside London
  • Clear published timelines ahead of each electoral cycle
  • Transparent reporting of diaspora turnout

More priorities will be added here as the chapter gathers member feedback. Have an issue you want raised with the national party? Tell the chapter.
