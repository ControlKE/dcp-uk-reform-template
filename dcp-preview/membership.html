Eligibility

  • Be a Kenyan citizen.
  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Be resident in the United Kingdom.
  • Accept the DCP constitution and values.
  • Provide complete, accurate information.
  • Pay the £20 registration fee.

What membership gives you

  • Party news and official statements direct to your inbox.
  • Invitations to UK town halls, meetups and chapter events.
  • A say in chapter matters that are put to members.
  • A route to raise diaspora issues with the national party.

Rights to vote in internal party elections are governed by the party constitution and by Kenyan electoral law, and may differ for members living abroad.

Please note. Registration and payment do not automatically confirm membership. Applications may be reviewed and verified before final approval.
What you are joining. This registers you as a member of the Democracy for the Citizens Party, a political party registered in Kenya. It is not membership of any United Kingdom political party, it confers no rights in United Kingdom elections, and it carries no affiliation with any UK party. Your details are processed by the national party under its privacy policy.
