Thousands of Kenyans build their lives in Britain and still carry Kenya with them — in the money they send, the families they support and the country they want to come home to. DCP UK is where that voice joins the party.

JOIN DCP UK → WHAT DCP STANDS FOR
About this chapter. DCP UK is the United Kingdom outreach chapter of the Democracy for the Citizens Party, a political party registered in Kenya on 3 February 2025 under Certificate Number 103. It is not a political party registered in the United Kingdom, it does not stand candidates in UK elections, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any United Kingdom political party.

OUR APPROACH

A Party That Listens

DCP is a citizen-centred political party committed to listening, justice, accountability, inclusion, unity and national progress. Our symbol — the listening ear — is our promise to every Kenyan: your voice will shape the future of this nation. Distance does not cancel that promise.
