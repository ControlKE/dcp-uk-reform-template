About DCP

A political party built to listen.

Overview

Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) was officially registered in Kenya on 3 February 2025, under Certificate Number 103. We exist to give every Kenyan — regardless of county, community or background — a seat at the table of national decision-making.

Vision

“A democratic and prosperous Kenya where citizens are respected, heard and meaningfully involved in shaping the nation’s future.”

Mission

“To advance leadership that listens to citizens, includes diverse communities and empowers Kenyans to participate in national development.”

Motto & Slogan

Motto: Justice, Unity and Progress
Slogan: Skiza Wakenya

The Listening Ear

Our symbol is a listening ear — a daily reminder that the DCP exists to hear Wakenya first and act second. Every policy we advance begins with listening.

DCP in the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is home to one of the largest Kenyan communities outside East Africa. Those Kenyans remain citizens: they hold Kenyan passports, support families at home, send money every month and, in many cases, intend to return. The party’s commitment to listening does not stop at the border.

DCP UK exists so that community has a named, organised route into the party — somewhere to register as a member, meet other members in their own city, and raise the issues that affect Kenyans abroad. It carries those issues to the national party rather than setting policy of its own.

The chapter is an outreach and membership body. All party policy, leadership and candidate selection remain matters for the national party in Kenya, under the party constitution.

What the chapter does

  • Registers Kenyan citizens living in the UK as party members.
  • Runs town halls, listening sessions and members’ meetups in UK cities.
  • Gathers diaspora views on national policy and passes them to the party.
  • Keeps members informed of party news, documents and decisions.

What the chapter is not

DCP UK is not a United Kingdom political party. It is not registered with the UK Electoral Commission, it does not contest United Kingdom elections at any level, it does not campaign in them, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any United Kingdom political party or movement. It is the diaspora chapter of a political party registered in Kenya.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The values that lead us

Integrity

Ethical, transparent leadership at every level.

Justice

Fair treatment and equal opportunity for every citizen.

Unity

One Kenya — bridging communities, regions and generations.

Accountability

Answerable to the people we serve, always.

Citizen Participation

Decisions shaped by the voices of Wakenya.

Inclusive Development

Progress that reaches every county and household.

Governance Structure

  • Party Leader and Deputy Party Leader
  • National Chairperson and Secretary-General
  • National Executive Committee (NEC)
  • National Governing Council (NGC)
  • County and Constituency committees across all 47 counties
  • Diaspora chapters, including the United Kingdom
