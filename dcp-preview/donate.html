Support DCP UK

Fund the chapter's own UK activities — town halls, listening sessions and outreach to Kenyans across Britain.

Make a donation

This is separate from the one-time £20 membership registration fee paid to the national party. Donations here support the UK chapter's own running costs — venue hire for town halls, travel for chapter volunteers, printing and outreach materials.

How donations are paid. Donations are made by bank transfer straight to the chapter's UK bank account. This page does not take card payments and never asks for your card or bank login details.

Thank you for your support

Please use the reference exactly as shown so the chapter can match your transfer to this pledge.

Where donations go

Chapter-level costs only: UK town halls and listening sessions, travel for volunteer coordinators, printing, and member communications. Donations do not fund national party operations in Kenya — those are handled separately by the national party.

Before this goes live

DCP UK is not a UK-registered political party, so standard UK political-donation rules for registered parties may not directly apply — but receiving donations from the UK into a foreign political party's accounts can still raise its own compliance questions (e.g. anti-money-laundering checks, the payment provider's own rules on political organisations, and any Kenyan-side reporting requirements). This template doesn't attempt to answer that — it's worth the chapter checking with a qualified adviser before taking real donations.
